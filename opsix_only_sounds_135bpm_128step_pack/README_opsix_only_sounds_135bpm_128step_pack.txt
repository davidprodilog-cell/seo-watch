Korg opsix-only MIDI pack - 135 BPM - 128 steps
No drums in this pack. It is designed for opsix sound roles: drones, pads, leads, basses, stabs and transition textures.
Each separated MIDI is format 0, one track, 480 PPQ, 8 bars / 128 sixteenth-note steps.
The all-tracks reference is MIDI format 1 and contains all opsix parts together.

Patch guidance:
- DRONE: long release, feedback FM, slow filter/motion, low in the mix.
- PAD: juno-like warmth or glass FM chords, slow attack/release, reverb/delay.
- LEAD: cyber/cross-mod/metallic lead, portamento, pitch env, delay.
- BASS: opsix can layer digital FM bass under TD-3 or replace it for colder sections.
- STAB: short cyber rave chord or noise/sweep transition cue.
