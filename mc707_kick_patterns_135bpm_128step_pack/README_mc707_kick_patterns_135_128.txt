MC-707 kick MIDI patterns - 135 BPM - 128 steps

These files contain rhythm/velocity only: choose the actual kick sound in the MC-707 kit.

Kick sound suggestions:
- 909 heavy/saturated for straight rave and free-party patterns.
- Short dry kick for dense TD-3 acid gallop.
- Bigger rounded kick for big-beat/breakbeat support.
- Reduce ghost kick velocity or delete ghost hits if the groove gets too crowded.
