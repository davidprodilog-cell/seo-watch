TD-3 acid gallop / frenzy MIDI pack - 135 BPM - 128 steps
Original dense acid patterns for a galloping/rising 303-style feel; not copied from any specific track.
Designed for MC-707 sequencing into TD-3 or TD-3-MO. Each file is MIDI format 0, one track, 480 PPQ, 8 bars / 128 sixteenth-note steps.

TD-3 starting point:
- Saw for bite or square for hollow gallop.
- Cutoff 9-10h at start, open over 8 bars; resonance 2-3h; env mod 2h; decay short around 9-10h; accent high.
- These are denser than the big-beat pack: use with strong kick, short decay and careful low-end level.
