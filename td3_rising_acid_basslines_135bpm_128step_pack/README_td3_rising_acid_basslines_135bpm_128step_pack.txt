TD-3 rising acid bassline MIDI pack - 135 BPM - 128 steps
Original rising acid/tekno basslines inspired by the idea of a climbing acid hook, not copied from any existing track.
Each MIDI is format 0, one track, 480 PPQ, 8 bars / 128 sixteenth-note steps.

Use with MC-707 -> TD-3:
- Send each MIDI to the TD-3 on its MIDI channel.
- Start TD-3 around: saw, cutoff 9-11h, resonance 1-3h, env mod 12-3h, decay short/medium, accent high.
- The MIDI includes velocity accents, portamento CC hints, cutoff/resonance CC ramps, and slide-style overlapping notes.
- For the rising feel, open cutoff gradually through the 8-bar cycle and push accent/distortion near the end.
