TD-3 big-beat / rave acid bassline MIDI pack - 135 BPM - 128 steps
Original MIDI patterns inspired by aggressive 90s big-beat/rave acid energy; not copied from any specific track.
Designed for MC-707 sequencing into TD-3 or TD-3-MO. Each file is MIDI format 0, one track, 480 PPQ, 8 bars / 128 sixteenth-note steps.

TD-3 starting point:
- Saw or square; cutoff around 9-11h, resonance 1-3h, env mod 12-3h, short decay, strong accent.
- For peak sections, open cutoff gradually over the 8 bars and add distortion.
- These lines are more riff/hook oriented than the Spiral packs, with stronger gaps for breakbeat-style drums.
